Xiaoyu Tom Chen

ML safety and reliability, and the engineering to go with it.

Ann Arbor, Michigan
xiaoyc@umich.edu

I work on why machine learning systems fail, and I build the systems that let me find out. Research half of the time, shipping the other half.

I am a Computer Science undergraduate at the University of Michigan, transferred from the University of Florida, graduating May 2028. Two research threads keep me busy. At the TEA Lab (UF ECE, Citi-sponsored) I own the data pipeline for an LLM agent safety benchmark. At OrderLab (UMich EECS) I work on AdaptGuard, an evaluation of sandbox policies for coding agents. Both come down to the same question: when an agent looks like it is behaving, how do you tell whether it actually is?

The other half is building. Muted is an AI-first English speaking practice app I designed, built and shipped on my own. Hoopalytics is a sorting-algorithm comparison engine over scraped NBA data. CRetor is a Mandarin counter-speech dataset built for fine-tuning. I like the parts where a clean idea meets a messy constraint, like discovering a voice API that works beautifully and costs far too much.

Away from the terminal I collect languages, seven of them at wildly uneven levels, and I have a long-running habit of memorising things that do not need memorising.
