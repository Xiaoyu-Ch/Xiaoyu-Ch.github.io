Xiaoyu Tom Chen

CS student, University of Michigan. Building production ML/systems infrastructure and LLM agent safety research.

Ann Arbor, MI

I’m a CS student at the University of Michigan (transferred from the University of Florida), building production ML and systems infrastructure. I think of myself as an engineer first: most of my time goes into shipping and stress-testing real systems, not just writing about them.

Right now I split my time between two things. At the TEA Lab (UF ECE, Citi-sponsored), I own the full data pipeline for FinVault, an LLM agentic-safety benchmark: single-pass logprob and entropy extraction on Qwen3-8B, run at scale (1,064 cases across 31 scenarios) on dual-H200 HPC. At OrderLab (University of Michigan EECS), I work on AdaptGuard, an evaluation harness for adaptive safety policies in coding agents, targeting OSDI 2026.

Outside the lab, I build things end to end: Muted, an AI-first English speaking practice app I designed, built, and shipped solo; Hoopalytics, a sorting-algorithm comparison engine over scraped NBA data; and CRetor, a Mandarin counter-speech dataset built for LLM fine-tuning.

I’m interested in both PhD study and industry roles in software engineering and ML infrastructure. My CV has the full picture, and my publications page has citations. If you want to reach me, email is best, or find me on GitHub and LinkedIn.
