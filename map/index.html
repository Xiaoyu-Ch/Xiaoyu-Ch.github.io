  3. 3 Gainesville, Florida

    August 2024 to May 2026

    B.S. in Computer Science at the University of Florida, 3.92 GPA. Gainesville is where research stopped being an idea and became a schedule.

    From February 2026 I worked in the Computer Vision and Sensing Lab at UF ABE on the perception pipeline for an autonomous bamboo-harvesting robot, part of a twenty-person team. From May 2026 I joined the TEA Lab at UF ECE as a research assistant on a Citi-sponsored LLM agent safety benchmark, where I own the data pipeline. I still work with the TEA Lab remotely.

    • University of Florida
    • TEA Lab
    • Computer Vision and Sensing Lab

  4. 4 Ann Arbor, Michigan

    August 2026 to present

    Transferred to the University of Michigan, graduating May 2028. Currently taking EECS 281 and EECS 370, which is a lot of data structures and a lot of caches.

    Since July 2026 I have been an undergraduate researcher at OrderLab in UMich EECS, advised by Prof. Ryan Huang, working on AdaptGuard: an evaluation of what sandbox policies actually do to a coding agent, and where they get it wrong in both directions.

    • University of Michigan
    • OrderLab
