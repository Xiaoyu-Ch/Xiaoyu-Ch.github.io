    How it is put together

    Every word in the bank is checked against the spelling shown, accents included. That is why Portuguese esquisito appears and Spanish exquisito does not: they are different words with opposite meanings, and the game would be lying if it treated them as one. Where a spelling really is shared, as with sale in English, Spanish and French, every one of those languages scores.

    Twenty-seven words in the bank, ten per game, drawn without replacement. Keyboard play: number keys 1 to 7 pick a language, Enter moves on. Runs client-side with no network requests and no storage.
