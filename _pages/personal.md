### Languages, and where that comes from

My father is a language scholar (he's held positions at ECNU, UNC, and UAlbany), and growing up around that shaped how I think about language, both human and machine. It's part of why I care about controllability and interpretability in LLMs, not just performance: I'm interested in systems that communicate the way people actually do. Outside of Mandarin and English, I've spent time with Spanish, French, German, Japanese, and Portuguese at varying levels, and I keep chipping away at all of them.

### Community and outside interests

I've volunteered with my university's Cooperative Living Organization (CLO), spent time tutoring and studying at language studios, and I still make time for basketball when I can. None of it is resume material, but all of it is part of how I spend my time.
